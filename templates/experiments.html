LAB 01

Pendulum: What controls the period?

Objective

Discover how pendulum length and gravity affect the time for one complete swing.

Independent variable

Length (or gravity when intentionally changed).

Dependent variable

Period, measured in seconds.

Controls

Keep the starting angle small when comparing lengths.

Before touching the controls: predict whether a longer pendulum will swing faster, slower, or at the same rate. Then test your claim.
