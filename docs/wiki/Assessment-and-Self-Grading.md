# Assessment and Self-Grading

The application is designed to automate grading wherever the answer can be evaluated reliably.

## Automatically gradable work

* multiple choice;
* vocabulary;
* definition matching;
* premise/conclusion identification;
* fallacy recognition;
* reading comprehension;
* structured physics questions;
* completion and retry logic.

## Structured writing

Open writing should not be reduced to a single opaque score. The application can first verify structure:

* question present;
* proposition present;
* required proofs present;
* major premise present;
* minor premise present;
* conclusion present;
* counter-proposition present;
* restatement present.

Higher-level semantic quality can remain separately reviewable.

## Feedback

Good self-grading should explain *why* an answer is wrong, identify the competency involved, and direct the learner to the smallest useful remediation.

## Retakes

Retakes should use different questions when possible. Otherwise a learner may memorize an answer rather than master the underlying skill.
