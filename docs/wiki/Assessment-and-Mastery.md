# Assessment and Mastery

## Self-grading
Objective work is automatically graded: multiple choice, vocabulary, validity checks, fallacy identification, structured argument assembly, reading checks, and diagnostic items.

## Open-ended work
Writing and debate use transparent structural rubrics. Automated scores measure required components, evidence/citation use, counterargument, revision, and completeness; they do not claim to determine whether a philosophical conclusion is true.

## Mastery
The platform tracks skills independently and schedules spaced review for weak areas. Course completion and mastery are intentionally distinct.
