# Student Guide

## Daily workflow

1. Open the dashboard.
2. Complete the recommended activity.
3. Read definitions before taking a position.
4. Read or watch the lesson material.
5. Manipulate the experiment when provided.
6. Complete the knowledge check.
7. Read feedback.
8. Correct weak answers.
9. Complete argument or ELA work.
10. Check mastery.
11. Continue or review as directed.

## Debate rule

You are not required to agree with the proposition you are assigned.

You are required to understand it well enough to defend it accurately.

## Source rule

Do not claim that a source says something merely because it supports your opinion. First identify what the source actually says.

## Experiment rule

Report what happened before explaining why you think it happened.

## Mastery rule

Passing once is evidence of learning. Applying the same skill successfully to a new problem is stronger evidence of mastery.
