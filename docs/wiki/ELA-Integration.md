# ELA Integration

Physics & Reality Tutor can serve as substantial ELA practice because reasoning is inseparable from language.

## Reading

Students read public-domain primary sources rather than only summaries. Reading assignments can measure main idea, vocabulary in context, inference, author's proposition, evidence, comparison, and summary.

## Writing

Students write:

* definitions;
* propositions;
* summaries;
* syllogisms;
* evidence explanations;
* counterarguments;
* rebuttals;
* experiment observations;
* explanatory paragraphs;
* argumentative essays.

## Speaking and debate

Oral debate exercises practice organized speech, listening comprehension, accurate restatement, rebuttal, and use of evidence.

## Cross-disciplinary rubric

A physics response can receive separate scores for scientific accuracy and written reasoning. A learner may understand the physical concept but need ELA remediation, or write clearly while misunderstanding the physics.

Keeping those dimensions separate makes mastery data more useful.
